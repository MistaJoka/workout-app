# Short cues for the starter moves: drafts

**Date:** 2026-10-05
**Status:** DRAFT, awaiting owner approval. Nothing here is wired into the app.

## Why

The nine original starter moves show the long free-exercise-db paragraphs in
the player and on their detail screen. The 30 Rae cards use four short cues
instead ("Feet flat", "Return with control"). These drafts give the starter
moves the same four-line style.

## Rule

- Each cue only shortens a line the licensed source already says. The source
  is `foundationStrengthStarter.ts` (free-exercise-db, Unlicense). Nothing new
  is added, and no safety rule is invented.
- The source line for each cue sits beside it.
- Once approved, the cues go on each move as `cues`. The player and the detail
  screen then show them first, with the full steps one tap away.

## Drafts

### Bodyweight Squat (`fs.bodyweight-squat`)
1. **Feet shoulder width.** Source: "Stand with your feet shoulder width apart."
2. **Sit your hips back.** Source: "flexing your knees and hips, sitting back with your hips."
3. **Chest up, knees out.** Source: "keep your head and chest up and push your knees out."
4. **Rise back up.** Source: "reverse the motion until you return to the starting position."

### Incline Push-Up (`fs.incline-push-up`)
1. **Hands on the bench edge.** Source: "Place hands on edge of bench or platform, slightly wider than shoulder width."
2. **Body straight.** Source: "Keeping body straight."
3. **Lower chest to the edge.** Source: "lower chest to edge of box or platform by bending arms."
4. **Push until arms are straight.** Source: "Push body up until arms are extended."

### Single Leg Glute Bridge (`fs.single-leg-glute-bridge`)
1. **Feet flat, knees bent.** Source: "Lay on the floor with your feet flat and knees bent."
2. **One knee to your chest.** Source: "Raise one leg off of the ground, pulling the knee to your chest."
3. **Drive through the heel.** Source: "driving through the heel, extending your hip upward."
4. **Pause, then lower.** Source: "pause and then return to the starting position."

### Dead Bug (`fs.dead-bug`)
1. **Knees and hips at 90°.** Source: "Bring your feet, knees, and hips up to 90 degrees."
2. **Back flat on the floor.** Source: "flatten your back onto the floor … Hold this position throughout."
3. **Reach one leg out.** Source: "extending one leg … to bring the leg just above the ground."
4. **Return, switch sides.** Source: "return the working leg … Repeat on the opposite side."

### Plank (`fs.plank`)
1. **Forearms under shoulders.** Source: "Your arms are bent and directly below the shoulder."
2. **Up on your toes.** Source: "supporting your weight on your toes and your forearms."
3. **Body straight.** Source: "Keep your body straight at all times."
4. **Hold.** Source: "hold this position."

### Walking Lunge (`fs.walking-lunge`)
1. **Hands on hips.** Source: "your hands on your hips."
2. **Step forward, drop your hips.** Source: "Step forward with one leg, flexing the knees to drop your hips."
3. **Front knee over the foot.** Source: "your front knee should stay above the front foot."
4. **Drive up through the heel.** Source: "Drive through the heel of your lead foot … to raise yourself back up."

### Glute Bridge (`fs.glute-bridge`)
1. **Knees bent, hands by your sides.** Source: "hands by your side and your knees bent."
2. **Push through your heels.** Source: "Pushing mainly with your heels, lift your hips off the floor."
3. **Hold at the top.** Source: "hold at the top for a second."
4. **Lower slowly.** Source: "Slowly go back to the starting position."

### Crunches (`fs.crunches`)
1. **Hands lightly by your head.** Source: "place your hands lightly on either side of your head."
2. **Low back stays down.** Source: "your lower back should remain on the floor."
3. **Shoulders up a few inches.** Source: "Your shoulders should come up off the floor only about four inches."
4. **Come down slowly.** Source: "begin to come down slowly again."

### Superman (`fs.superman`)
1. **Face down, arms long.** Source: "lie straight and face down … arms should be fully extended in front of you."
2. **Lift arms, legs and chest.** Source: "raise your arms, legs, and chest off of the floor."
3. **Hold 2 seconds.** Source: "hold this contraction for 2 seconds."
4. **Lower slowly.** Source: "Slowly begin to lower your arms, legs and chest."
