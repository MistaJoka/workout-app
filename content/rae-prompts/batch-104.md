**7. Power Stairs** (Side view, 4 frames; equipment: whatever equipment the steps describe, drawn simply)
Frames: 1) start position, 2) halfway, 3) end of the movement (peak), 4) halfway back.
Steps: In the power stairs, implements are moved up a staircase. For training purposes, these can be performed with a tire or box. Begin by taking the implement with both hands.
