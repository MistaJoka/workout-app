**1. Cross Body Hammer Curl** (Side view, 4 frames; equipment: dumbbells (one if the steps use one arm))
Frames: 1) start position, 2) halfway, 3) end of the movement (peak), 4) halfway back.
Steps: Stand up straight with a dumbbell in each hand. Your hands should be down at your side with your palms facing in. Frames: 1) start position, 2) halfway, 3) end of the movement (peak), 4) halfway back.

Frames: 1) start position, 2) halfway, 3) end of the movement (peak), 4) halfway back.
Steps: Stand up straight with a dumbbell in each hand at arm's length. Keep your elbows close to your torso and rotate the palms of your hands until they are facing forward.
